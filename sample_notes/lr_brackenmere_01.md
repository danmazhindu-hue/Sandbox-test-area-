# Search exercise at the fen

Lowland searches start with a briefing in the car park and a laminated map of the search area divided into numbered sectors. Teams are told which sector, which bearing and how many metres apart to walk.

The search manager, Dev, repeats the same safety briefing before every task even though everyone can recite it. He said that if he ever stopped, someone would assume the rule had changed.

Gwen is a retired nurse and the team's unofficial first-aider. Younger members defer to her over the formal first-aid lead, who is a paramedic. Gwen said she does not mind as long as nobody asks her to write anything down.

Teams carry a tablet that logs the track of each walker. Dev said the track logs have changed arguments about whether an area was searched, but the tablets die in the cold and paper sector sheets are still filled in as backup.

On the sheet, the missing person is categorised by age and behaviour, such as "dementia, wanderer" or "teenager, low risk". Gwen said she dislikes the shorthand but accepts that the search plan depends on it.
