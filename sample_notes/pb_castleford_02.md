# Joint debrief

The debrief was held at the police station with tea and biscuits. Volunteers sat on one side of the table and officers on the other without anyone suggesting it.

An officer said the volunteers are better at the long search but "can't be told what to do". A team leader said the police are better at the first hour but "can't be told anything".

The sergeant with the search qualification was quoted twice by other officers as the person to ask. Nobody mentioned her rank.

A shared messaging group was started after the debrief so that team leaders and the sergeant can reach each other quickly. Several people asked who would be allowed to see it.
