# Control room and a missing person job

In Force B the risk grade has an extra category, so a medium can be marked "medium plus". A call handler said this is an unofficial fix that crept in because high felt too dramatic.

The inspector said she calls the volunteer teams "partners" in public and "the volunteers" in the office. The difference was noticeable to the people who heard both.

The force has a procedure for requesting search teams, but in practice the log shows requests going to whoever the sergeant has the number for. This was described to me as "knowing who to ring".

The control room uses a mapping system that shows the last known position and every team's reported track. One operator said watching the dots is the part of the job she finds hardest to switch off.

Post-incident the force sends a form to team leaders asking how the job went. Two leaders told me they have never filled one in.
