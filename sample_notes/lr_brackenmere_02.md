# Callout, missing dog walker

The call came through the police control room, not the team's own system. Dev spent ten minutes on the phone agreeing what the team would and would not do before anyone drove to the fen.

The police inspector asked for "volunteers with experience of the area". Dev answered by naming three people, none of whom hold a formal rank.

The police gave a risk grading of "medium" for the missing walker. The team's own view, written on the whiteboard, was different, and Dev said that gap is the thing newcomers take longest to learn.

Handover to the police at the end of the search was a single form signed on the bonnet of a car. The police officer photographed the form with a phone.
