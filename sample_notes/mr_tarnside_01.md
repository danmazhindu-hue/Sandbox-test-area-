# Valley team base visit

The Tarnside base is a converted barn. The kettle has a rota stuck to it that is taken more seriously than the callout procedure.

Here the callout form is a text message, not a pager. The team leader reads out the message and then asks anyone who disagrees with the plan to say so now. It is a quiet version of the Aldermoor ritual and several members said they prefer it.

Marta calls herself "just a volunteer" but has run the radio room for ten years. The police liaison officer always goes to her first when a job starts, not to the team leader.

The team uses a shared map app. One member said they had stopped carrying a compass entirely, which prompted a long argument about what counts as being competent.

Jobs are labelled in the log as "vulnerable misper", "fall", "medical" or "animal". Marta said the labels come from the police and are not how the team thinks about them.
