# Training weekend

The training weekend started with a half-hour on casualty care. The trainer insisted we write the observations on the glove, not the hand, in case the glove had to be cut off.

Kit checks are done in pairs against a laminated list. The list is revised every winter and the old copies are binned in front of everyone, which Priya said is so no one uses the old one by mistake.

Priya is the team's only qualified helicopter liaison. She is not an officer but nobody else is allowed to wave the aircraft in. Her expertise is respected in a way that rank is not.

A new drone was trialled on the ridge. The pilot kept the screen shielded and said the thermal view turns every sheep into a casualty. Opinions were split on whether it will ever replace a line of walkers.

The trainer drew four boxes on the whiteboard for sorting callouts: lost, injured, stuck, and "other". Most members laughed at "other" but agreed it is where the interesting jobs go.
